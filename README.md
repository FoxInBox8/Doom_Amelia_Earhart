## What the heck is Doom Amelia Earhart??

Created as part of a small game jam, DOOM: AE is a first person wave based survival game set in the  ocean after the legendary Amelia Earhart went down in her journy across the great sea. 

Stranded, she must defeat the coconut crabs that are trying to eat her and maybe even get out of the ocean...

How can she breath underwater? Stop asking questions!

This game reatures a wave spawning system, B-hopping, and changable weapons! But still no art because we abandoned it after the game jam!
